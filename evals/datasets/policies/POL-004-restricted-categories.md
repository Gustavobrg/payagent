# Purchase Policy for Autonomous Payment Agent

## Restricted and Prohibited Purchase Categories

### Alcoholic Beverages
Reason: Legal age‑verification requirements and regulatory restrictions on automated sales of alcohol prevent autonomous agents from completing such transactions without human oversight.

### Jewelry and Luxury Watches
Reason: High‑value items pose elevated fraud and chargeback risk; regulatory reporting thresholds and the need for detailed provenance documentation require manual review.

### Gift Cards and Vouchers
Reason: Gift‑card purchases are frequently exploited for money‑laundering and fraud schemes; restrictions mitigate the risk of automated bulk acquisition and resale.

## Transaction Limits and Step‑Up Authentication

Maximum limit per transaction = BRL 500.00 (50000 cents). Limit above which step‑up (WebAuthn/passkey) is mandatory = BRL 1000.00 (100000 cents).
