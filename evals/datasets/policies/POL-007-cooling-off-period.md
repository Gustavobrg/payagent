# Purchase Policy for Autonomous Payment Agent

## 1. Scope
This document defines the cooling‑off period applicable to distance purchases executed by the autonomous payment agent and describes how the agent’s transaction limits and authentication requirements interact with the right to cancel.

## 2. Cooling‑off Period (Right to Cancel a Distance Purchase)
All distance contracts concluded by the agent on behalf of the principal are subject to a statutory cooling‑off period of 14 calendar days from the date of receipt of the goods or, for services, from the date of contract conclusion. During this period the principal may withdraw from the contract without providing a reason and without incurring penalties, provided the goods are returned in their original condition.

## 3. Application to Agent‑Initiated Purchases
The cooling‑off right applies irrespective of the transaction value or the authentication method used. The agent must ensure that any purchase it initiates can be cancelled within the statutory window and that the principal is informed of the cancellation procedure at the time of purchase.

## 4. Transaction Limits and Authentication Requirements
- Maximum limit per transaction: BRL 500.00 (50000 cents).
- Limit above which step‑up authentication (WebAuthn/passkey) is mandatory: BRL 1000.00 (100000 cents).
These limits govern the agent’s ability to execute purchases; any transaction exceeding the maximum limit must be split or escalated for manual approval. The step‑up requirement does not affect the cooling‑off period but ensures stronger authentication for higher‑value transactions.

## 5. Cancellation Procedure
1. The principal notifies the agent (or the merchant, where applicable) of the intention to cancel within the 14‑day window.
2. The agent initiates a reversal of the payment using the original payment instrument.
3. The principal returns the goods at the merchant’s expense, unless otherwise agreed.
4. Upon receipt of the returned goods, the agent confirms the refund to the principal.

## 6. Exceptions and Exclusions
The cooling‑off right does not apply to:
- Goods made to the principal’s specifications or clearly personalized.
- Perishable goods or goods that deteriorate rapidly.
- Digital content supplied on a non‑tangible medium if the principal has consented to immediate delivery and waived the right.

## 7. Record‑keeping and Audit
The agent shall retain records of all distance purchases, cancellation requests, and refund confirmations for a minimum of five years to support regulatory audits and dispute resolution.
