# Purchase Policy: Refund Conditions for Settled Transactions

## Scope
This policy defines the general conditions under which a refund may be requested for a purchase that has already been settled by the autonomous payment agent. It applies to all transactions processed through the agent, regardless of payment method or merchant category.

## Eligibility
A refund request is eligible when:
- The transaction has been fully settled and the funds have been transferred to the merchant.
- The request is initiated within the time frame specified by the merchant’s return policy and applicable consumer protection regulations.
- The goods or services have not been consumed, or the consumer can demonstrate a valid defect, non‑delivery, or misrepresentation.

## Refund Request Procedure
1. The consumer submits a refund request through the agent’s designated interface, providing the transaction identifier, reason for refund, and any supporting documentation.
2. The agent validates the request against the merchant’s refund policy and the eligibility criteria outlined above.
3. Upon validation, the agent initiates the refund process and notifies both the consumer and the merchant.

## Amount Limits and Authentication Requirements
- The maximum refund amount per transaction shall not exceed **BRL 500.00 (50000 cents)**.
- For refund requests exceeding **BRL 1000.00 (100000 cents)**, step‑up authentication using WebAuthn/passkey is mandatory before the refund can be processed.

## Processing Timeline
Refunds that meet all eligibility and authentication requirements will be processed within five (5) business days from the date of request approval. The actual credit to the consumer’s account may depend on the financial institution’s processing times.

## Exceptions
Refunds may be denied or delayed in the following circumstances:
- The request falls outside the merchant’s stated return window.
- The goods have been used, damaged, or altered by the consumer.
- The transaction involves digital goods or services that have been accessed or downloaded.
- Regulatory or legal restrictions prohibit the refund.

## Governing Law
This policy is governed by the laws of the Federative Republic of Brazil and shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable consumer protection statutes.
